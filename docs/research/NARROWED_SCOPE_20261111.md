## Bounded handoff

S0 uses zero forwards, training or chemistry. S1/S2 may use analytic fixtures,
fake/cached references and, after hash checks, at most 192 frozen-model forwards,
12 JVPs/24 backward operations, three saved states, 600 CPU/wall seconds, zero GPU
and zero QC. All stages have explicit 256 MiB input/16 MiB output limits,
concurrency one and zero retries. Tests have a separate software-only envelope.
Stop at the first limit; failed work remains charged. Missing artifacts give a
partial report, not a physical rerun.

The old geoinfo rows contain event/score summaries but no intermediate geometry
tensors. The only permitted reconstruction uses the pinned input/noise/model
and existing event-lead2 50-step integrator, saving steps 10/25/40 before response
measurement. This creates diagnostic states; it is not falsely described as
loading archived states. Float64 CPU derivative diagnostics are distinct from
the historical float32 GPU execution. They cannot certify chemistry.

S1 audits amplitude and endpoint transport separately. S2 fixes SO(3) and current
calculator identity and checks decoded candidate mapping. S3 repairs started
failure charging and projects old stage evidence without invoking a certifier.
S4 asks a new, narrow question: **do the corrected identities/accounting and
measured endpoint/BE response supply evidence for the exact strict-channel
endpoint that the old catalogue studies could not establish?** It does not
repeat head training, trajectories or QC. If the data cannot identify that
increment, the conclusion is inconclusive and S5 stays closed.

Main outcome is unique independently connected channels per parent at a fixed
vector budget. Ranking, stationary-point quality and numerical response remain
separate endpoints. All attempted/invalid/unresolved/budget-limited candidates
stay in denominators; parents are paired units and families define clustering.
For any future efficacy protocol, a provisional practical effect is +0.10 in
per-parent probability of at least one strict connection, with a paired
family-aware 95% interval excluding zero for promotion and an upper bound below
+0.10 for practical exclusion. This threshold is a planning criterion, not
retroactive evidence; no single-parent diagnostic can meet that inference gate.

S5 requires legal inputs, stable numerics, chemical handoff, complete charging
and S4 endpoint-specific signal or a separately frozen discriminating plan.
Missing independent input/provenance, a merely changed score, or an inconclusive
report cannot waive those gates. S6 writing may proceed directly after S4;
confirmation computation additionally requires S5 and independently unexposed
data. All PRs remain draft and unmerged for human review.
